# Business Process

## Lifecycle

| Stage | Typical responsibility |
|---|---|
| Initiation | HR creates the onboarding record and confirms core details |
| Planning | Required department task templates are selected |
| Assignment | Tasks are generated and assigned to responsible teams |
| Execution | Departments update status, notes and completion evidence |
| Monitoring | HR and management review progress and blockers |
| Completion | Required tasks are validated and onboarding is closed |

## Example Task Areas

- Direct-manager preparation
- User-account creation
- Hardware allocation
- Network and application access
- Security review
- Operational readiness
- HR completion and administrative activities

## Status Model

A controlled model may use statuses such as Not Started, In Progress, Blocked, Completed and Not Applicable. Overall onboarding status should be calculated from required tasks rather than manually inferred.
